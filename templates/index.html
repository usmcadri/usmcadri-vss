Visual Snow Syndrome (VSS) is a neurological condition characterised by a persistent "static" overlay across the visual field, along with symptoms such as after-images, light sensitivity, and tinnitus.

Adjust the preset, intensity, and text settings in the panel on the left to find a combination that reduces eye strain for you. The colour overlay simulates the tinted lenses (such as NoIR or FL-41) used by many people with light-sensitive conditions.
